BTU Bricks by Wood Waste Industries

A clean, all-wood alternative to firewood for stoves, fireplaces and wood heating – available through Chris Miller.

BTU Bricks are pressed and formed entirely from sawdust and wood shavings – no additives, no glues, no fillers. Each brick weighs about 2 pounds and burns like seasoned firewood with a fraction of the mess.

Why an all-wood brick?

  • Made 100% from sawdust and wood shavings
  • Burns clean, dense and long – a great firewood alternative
  • Neat, easy-to-stack storage with no splitting or hauling
  • Built for stoves, fireplaces and wood-heating appliances

Chris Miller carries BTU Bricks for customers across New England. Pick some up while you’re planning your next paver project, or ask for availability and pricing.
