About Chris

A reputation built on satisfied customers

Through quality workmanship, attention to detail and prompt, courteous service, Chris Miller has built a solid reputation of customer satisfaction across the New England area for 16+ years.

Chris Miller is a New England brick paver installer based in Pelham, New Hampshire. Since 2010 he has specialized in brick paver installation – driveways, walkways, patios, pool decks and rock & block retaining walls – for homeowners across New Hampshire and Massachusetts.

The work stands on three simple promises: quality workmanship, attention to detail, and prompt, courteous service. From the first visit to the final sweep of the jointing sand, Chris treats every property like his own.

Free estimates, real answers

Every project begins with a free, no-pressure estimate. Chris walks the site with you, talks through materials and layout, and gives you an honest price before any work starts.
