Brick Driveways

Brick paver driveways give a home instant curb appeal while standing up to daily traffic and New England winters. Each one is installed on a proper compacted base so the pavers stay put for years, and every paver installation is backed by a 5-year warranty.

Walkways

From a straight front walk to winding paths through the yard, paver walkways connect your home with style. Chris lays out and installs walkways that fit the pitch and drainage of your property and hold their line season after season.

Patios

A paver patio turns the backyard into living space. Durable, low-maintenance and built on a solid base, patios are sized and patterned to fit how your family actually uses the space from cookouts to quiet mornings.

Pool Decks

Pool decks take constant moisture and bare feet. Pavers stay cooler than concrete, drain well and give a non-slip surface around the pool. Chris builds decks that are safe, comfortable and made to handle years of splash and sun.

Rock & Block Retaining Walls

Retaining walls keep slopes from sliding and turn unusable ground into usable space. Chris builds rock and block walls that are structurally sound and finished to complement the rest of your landscape.
